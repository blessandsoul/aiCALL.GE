<h2 id="what-outbound-does">What an Outbound Agent Does on Each Call</h2>

<p>An outbound call has a job: find out if this contact is worth a human's time, and if so, capture them. The agent runs that job consistently across the whole list.</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>It opens in Georgian.</strong> The agent states who is calling and why, in the caller's language, and switches to Russian or English if the person answers in one.</li>
  <li><strong>It qualifies.</strong> A short set of questions sorts a real prospect from a wrong number, so your team only sees contacts that fit.</li>
  <li><strong>It books or captures.</strong> When intent is high, it offers a callback or an appointment from the live calendar. When it is medium, it captures the number and reason for follow-up.</li>
  <li><strong>It logs everything.</strong> Each call lands in the CRM with the outcome, so the pipeline updates itself. The mechanics are in <a href="/blog/voice-agent-crm-logged-calls">every call logged to your CRM</a>.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="the-scale-math">The Scale a Single Operator Cannot Match</h2>

<p>One human SDR has a hard ceiling. A person can dial only so many numbers in a shift, gets tired by the afternoon, and leaves a large share of cold calls unanswered on the first try. The agent has no shift and no fatigue. It dials in parallel, retries unanswered numbers on a schedule you set, and keeps the same energy on call 300 as on call 3.</p>

<p>That changes the unit economics of prospecting. Instead of paying a rep to grind a list slowly, you point the agent at the list and let it surface the handful of warm leads worth a personal call. Your humans then spend their time closing, not dialing. <h2 id="worked-example-b2b">Worked Example: A B2B Supplier's Cold List</h2>

<p>A Tbilisi supplier sells fittings to construction firms. It has a spreadsheet of 600 contacts from trade events, sitting cold because the two-person sales team cannot work it and serve current clients at the same time. The list ages, and the leads cool.</p>

<p>The agent takes the list. Over a few days it calls every number, introduces the catalog, and asks two questions: are you sourcing fittings this quarter, and who handles purchasing. It books 18 callbacks with buyers who said yes, captures 40 more as medium-intent for a later touch, and marks the rest closed. The sales team opens its CRM to 18 warm conversations instead of 600 cold numbers. The supplier turned a dead spreadsheet into a working pipeline without hiring. The list-warming pattern is detailed in <a href="https://aichats.ge/en/blog/cold-list-to-warm-lead">cold list to warm lead</a>.</p>

<h2 id="staying-compliant">Staying Honest and Compliant</h2>

<p>Outbound calling carries rules, and the agent is built to respect them. It discloses that the caller is speaking with an AI. It works within your calling hours so it does not phone people at odd times. It captures consent and reason where your process requires it, and it honors a do-not-call signal by marking the contact and moving on.</p>

<p>This matters in Georgia, where personal data rules apply and trust is local. An agent that bulldozes through a list at midnight does more harm than good. One that calls in working hours, says it is AI, and leaves a clean record protects your brand while it works. The qualification logic that decides who reaches a human is in <a href="https://aichats.ge/en/blog/ai-lead-qualification-before-human">AI lead qualification before human</a>.</p>

<h2 id="faq">FAQ</h2>

<h3 id="faq-1">Can the outbound agent really replace a sales development rep?</h3>
<p>It replaces the dialing and first qualification, not the closing. The agent works the whole list, sorts warm from cold, and books callbacks, so your human reps spend their time on the prospects worth a personal conversation.</p>

<h3 id="faq-2">Does it call in Georgian?</h3>
<p>Yes. The agent opens in Georgian and switches to Russian or English mid-call if the contact answers in another language. The script and qualifying questions run in the caller's language.</p>

<h3 id="faq-3">Is outbound AI calling allowed in Georgia?</h3>
<p>Yes, with disclosure and within calling hours. The agent states that it is an AI, respects the hours you set, and honors a do-not-call signal. It logs each call so your records stay clean.</p>

<h3 id="faq-4">How are the results tracked?</h3>
<p>Every call writes to your CRM with its outcome: booked, captured for follow-up, or closed. The pipeline updates as the agent dials, so you read warm leads rather than a raw call log.</p>
